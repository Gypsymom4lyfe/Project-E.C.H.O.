## Outdoor Education & STEM Integration

### Why This Matters

**Expands Experiential Learning & STEM:** The Outdoor Education program at Newport News Park covers essential outdoor and physical skills — canoeing, archery, hiking, orienteering, team building, and environmental conservation. Combining this with ForKids' focus on hands-on STEM and summer enrichment gives under-resourced students access to transformative outdoor experiences.

**Breaches Access Barriers:** Programs held at Newport News Park often require specific registration steps, transportation coordination, and health/permission forms. Housing this information or enrollment reminders directly within an accessible app/portal helps working parents, guardians, and case managers navigate the process without missing key deadlines.

**Strengthens Regional Partnerships:** Connecting school district offerings (such as Newport News Public Schools' summer PE outdoor courses) with local non-profits like ForKids creates a stronger safety net of educational and recreational opportunities for kids across Hampton, Newport News, and surrounding cities.

---

### 💡 Potential App Features

- **Activity & Field Trip Schedules:** Push notifications for upcoming outdoor days, seasonal camps, and transportation shuttle pick-up points.
- **Interactive Checklist & Preparation:** Simple guides on what students need to bring (sunscreen, water bottles, proper footwear) for park days.
- **Digital Form Submission:** Streamlined access to required medical forms, physicals, and permission waivers right from a smartphone.
